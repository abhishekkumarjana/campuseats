# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food service system that allows students to view available food, place orders, and manage their food purchases through one system.

## Who

* **Students** – Browse food, place orders, make payments, and track orders.
* **Food Vendors** – Manage food items, menus, and student orders.
* **Administrator** – Manages users, vendors, and the overall system.
